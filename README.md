## Featured project

**[Flight Ops](https://github.com/Blacfalcon/flight-ops)**: flight operations management for a military helicopter unit. Every sortie goes through the same steps: serviceability, the mission order, readiness, risk and IMSAFE, clearance, the flight and the debrief, and the system won't let one be skipped. It runs as a website, installs on Android and iPhone with phone notifications, and keeps flight-line forms working with no signal.
